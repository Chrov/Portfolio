Chile’s copper: volume versus value

What should management monitor when export value grows faster than production?

In 2024 production rose 4.9%, nominal export value 16.7% and nominal LME price 7.9%. Export growth is not a production-growth metric.

Maintain separate physical-volume, price and export-value indicators. Investigate their divergence before revising operating capacity assumptions.

Demand planning · Synthetic simulation

A forecast a small business can operate

With only date, product and demand, can a transparent forecast improve on repeating last week?

The weekday mean reduces held-out WMAPE from 44.6% to 35.9%: a 19.4% relative reduction. 69 products enter volume class A, including the item crossing the 80% threshold.

Start with the weekday mean as a planning input. Collect stock availability, lead times and actual transactions before recommending reorder quantities or monetizing savings.

Banking · Historical academic dataset

When a banking model should not ship

Can contact capacity be prioritized using information available before a call—and does that ranking survive a later cohort?

Held-out AUC is 0.458. At 20% capacity the model captures 26.7% of observed subscribers, but the response rate shifts from 4.8% in training to 30.8% in hold-out. The positive top-capacity lift does not rescue overall ranking or calibration.

Do not deploy this model. Investigate cohort construction and macroeconomic drift, obtain recent pre-contact data and validate a new policy before an experiment. No campaign ROI or causal uplift is claimed.
